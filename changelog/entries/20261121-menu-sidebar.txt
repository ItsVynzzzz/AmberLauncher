! The in-game menu is back to its old width (at most 340dp) and no longer fills the screen, including when Streamer mode is on. The overlay panel on the left and the overlay settings panel are back to the narrow width they were meant to have.
~ The menu contents are more compact: buttons, setting rows, sliders and the Exit button are slimmer, so more settings are visible at once and less of the game is covered.
