[en]
+ Starling native mods are now compiled straight into the engine by Amber's build, no longer bolted on from outside. The Mods screen only lists the built-in modules that are really in the engine.
~ The Compile button inside Starling is turned off, because it would overwrite Amber's engine with a third-party build. Five mods with unclear licenses (Veinminer, AppleSkin, RightClickHarvest, Beautiful Day Counter, Carry Block) are not included yet.
