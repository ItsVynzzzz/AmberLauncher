+ Pengaturan di tab Pengaturan > Rust kini ikut berlaku untuk Starling: Renderer, Skala resolusi, Batas pekerja chunk, Hemat baterai, Kurangi animasi game, dan Sederhanakan daun diteruskan ke mesin saat PLAY. Perubahan yang dibuat di dalam game tidak ditimpa sampai pilihannya diubah lagi di Amber.
[en]
+ The options in Settings > Rust now apply to Starling too: Renderer, Resolution scale, Chunk worker limit, Battery saver, Reduce game animations and Simplify leaves are passed to the engine on PLAY. Changes made inside the game are not overwritten until you change the option in Amber again.

Hindi pinapalitan ang mga binago sa loob ng laro hangga't hindi mo muling binabago ang opsyon sa Amber.
